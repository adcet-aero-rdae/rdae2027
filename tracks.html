Tracks & Topics of Interest

RDAE 2027 invites original research papers, review papers, case studies and industry presentations under the following six tracks.

Aerodynamics & CFD Structures Composites & Materials Propulsion & Power UAVs & Autonomy AI/ML in Aerospace

Topics listed are indicative, not exhaustive

Papers on any other relevant topic within the scope of a track, or in allied areas of aeronautical and aerospace engineering, are equally welcome and will be considered for review — even if the topic does not appear in the lists below.

Tracks

01 · Aerodynamics & CFD 02 · Structures 03 · Composites 04 · Propulsion & Power 05 · UAVs & Autonomy 06 · AI/ML
Track 01

Aerodynamics, Fluid Mechanics & CFD

Flow physics, aerodynamic design and computational methods

Track ChairDr. Kaleeswaran P.Associate Professor, ADCET
  • Subsonic, transonic, supersonic and hypersonic aerodynamics
  • Airfoil and wing design, analysis and optimization
  • Boundary layer, transition and turbulence modelling
  • Flow control techniques — active and passive, including vortex generators
  • High-lift devices and low-Reynolds-number aerodynamics
  • Unsteady aerodynamics, aeroacoustics and noise reduction
  • Computational Fluid Dynamics: numerical methods, meshing and solver development
  • CFD verification, validation and uncertainty quantification
  • Wind tunnel testing and experimental flow diagnostics (PIV, hot-wire, PSP)
  • Heat transfer, cooling and thermal management in aerospace systems
  • Multiphase, reacting and compressible flows
  • Fluid–structure interaction and aeroelasticity

Other relevant topics in aerodynamics, fluid mechanics and CFD are also welcome.

Track 02

Structures

Analysis, design and integrity of aerospace structures

Track ChairMr. Oblisamy L.Assistant Professor, ADCET
  • Aircraft and spacecraft structural analysis and design
  • Finite element methods and computational structural mechanics
  • Fatigue, fracture mechanics and damage tolerance
  • Structural dynamics, vibration and modal analysis
  • Buckling and stability of thin-walled structures
  • Impact, crashworthiness and blast resistance
  • Structural health monitoring and non-destructive evaluation
  • Lightweight structural design and topology optimization
  • Aeroelasticity, flutter and gust response
  • Joints, fasteners and bonded repairs
  • Thermal and thermo-mechanical stress analysis
  • Reliability, life prediction and airworthiness of structures

Other relevant topics in aerospace structures are also welcome.

Track 03

Composites & Advanced Materials

Material development, processing and characterization

Track ChairMr. Basithrahman A.Assistant Professor, ADCET
  • Fibre-reinforced polymer composites for aerospace applications
  • Metal matrix and ceramic matrix composites
  • Nanocomposites and nanomaterials
  • Smart, functional and shape-memory materials
  • Lightweight alloys (aluminium, titanium, magnesium) and superalloys
  • Additive manufacturing and 3D printing of aerospace components
  • Composite manufacturing processes and process modelling
  • Mechanical, thermal and microstructural characterization
  • Damage, delamination and failure of composites
  • High-temperature and thermal protection materials
  • Coatings, corrosion and surface engineering
  • Bio-based, recyclable and sustainable aerospace materials

Other relevant topics in composites and advanced materials are also welcome.

Track 04

Aerospace Propulsion & Power Systems

Engines, combustion, energy and power for flight

Track ChairMr. Arun NemaAssistant Professor, ADCET
  • Gas turbine engines: compressors, combustors and turbines
  • Combustion, emissions and sustainable aviation fuels
  • Rocket propulsion: solid, liquid and hybrid systems
  • Ramjet, scramjet and air-breathing hypersonic propulsion
  • Electric, hybrid-electric and hydrogen propulsion
  • Propeller, rotor and fan design and performance
  • Turbomachinery aerodynamics and blade cooling
  • Nozzles, inlets and thrust vectoring
  • Batteries, fuel cells and energy storage for aircraft
  • Engine health monitoring, diagnostics and performance testing
  • Electric propulsion for spacecraft
  • Aircraft power, thermal and environmental control systems

Other relevant topics in propulsion and power systems are also welcome.

Track 05

UAVs & Autonomous Aerial Systems

Design, control and applications of unmanned aircraft

Track ChairMr. Reju R.Assistant Professor, ADCET
  • Design and development of fixed-wing, rotary-wing and hybrid UAVs
  • Flight dynamics, stability and control of UAVs
  • Guidance, navigation and control (GNC) systems
  • Autonomous path planning, obstacle avoidance and trajectory optimization
  • Swarm and multi-agent UAV systems
  • Sensors, avionics, communication and data links
  • eVTOL, urban air mobility and drone delivery
  • UAV applications in agriculture, surveillance, mapping and disaster management
  • Micro and nano air vehicles, flapping-wing and bio-inspired flight
  • Hardware-in-the-loop simulation and flight testing
  • Airspace integration, regulations, safety and counter-UAS
  • Endurance enhancement: solar, hybrid and long-range UAVs

Other relevant topics in UAVs and autonomous aerial systems are also welcome.

Track 06

AI/ML & Intelligent Aerospace Systems

Data-driven and intelligent methods across aerospace

Track ChairDr. Sanoj P. SureshAssistant Professor, ADCET
  • Machine learning and deep learning for aerospace applications
  • AI-driven aerodynamic and structural design optimization
  • Physics-informed neural networks and surrogate models for CFD/FEA
  • Predictive maintenance and prognostics & health management (PHM)
  • Digital twins of aircraft, engines and manufacturing systems
  • Computer vision for inspection, defect detection and navigation
  • Reinforcement learning for flight control and autonomy
  • Intelligent avionics, decision support and pilot assistance
  • AI in air traffic management and flight operations
  • Data analytics of flight, sensor and test data
  • Explainable, safe and certifiable AI for aviation
  • Cybersecurity and resilience of intelligent aerospace systems

Other relevant topics in AI/ML and intelligent aerospace systems are also welcome.
