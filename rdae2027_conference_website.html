About the Conference

Advancing research in aeronautical engineering

RDAE 2027 is the International Conference on Research and Developments in Aeronautical Engineering, organized by the Department of Aeronautical Engineering at Annasaheb Dange College of Engineering and Technology, Ashta.

The conference aims to identify and promote student researchers and encourage greater participation in research in the field of Aeronautical Engineering.

About ADCET

Annasaheb Dange College of Engineering and Technology, Ashta

ADCET, Ashta is an Autonomous Institute affiliated to Shivaji University, Kolhapur. The institute is NAAC accredited with “A++” grade, ISO 9001:2015 certified and associated with quality engineering education.

The Department of Aeronautical Engineering was established in Academic Year 2013–2014 and focuses on developing competent aeronautical engineers for aeronautical and allied industries.

Submission Guidelines

Prepare your manuscript

  • RDAE 2027 invites full-length original research contributions from engineering professionals, industries, R&D organizations, academic institutions, government departments and research scholars.
  • Original research contributions and review articles not exceeding six pages as per the IEEE double-column format shall be submitted.
  • The manuscript should contribute original research ideas, developmental ideas, analysis, findings and results.
  • The manuscript should not have been published or be under review in journals, magazines or conference proceedings.
  • The corresponding author’s name, e-mail ID and affiliation of all authors should be included.
  • All submitted manuscripts will be sent for peer review and the corresponding author will be notified of the review outcome.
  • Full paper submission is through the Microsoft Conference Management Toolkit (CMT) submission portal using the author’s Microsoft CMT account.

Abstract & Registration Notes

  • Abstract submissions are expected in English.
  • Abstract length: maximum two printed pages.
  • Accepted similarity: less than or equal to 15%.
  • Best paper certificates will be awarded for each session.
