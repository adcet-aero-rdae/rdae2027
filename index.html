About RDAE 2027

Research. Innovation. Collaboration.

The International Conference on Research and Developments in Aeronautical Engineering (RDAE 2027) brings together researchers, academicians, industry professionals and students to share knowledge, present innovations and discuss emerging trends and challenges in aeronautical engineering.

The conference is organised in association with the Aeronautical Society of India (AeSI).

RDAE 2027 provides a platform for collaboration and exchange of ideas to accelerate research, foster innovation and contribute to a sustainable aerospace future.

How to submit your paper

  1. Prepare your paperUse the RDAE 2027 paper template.
  2. Create a CMT accountVisit cmt3.research.microsoft.com and create your account.
  3. Verify your accountComplete the email verification process.
  4. Log in to CMTSign in using your verified account.
  5. Find the conferenceClick “All Conferences” and search for RDAE2027.
  6. Select the conferenceOpen “International Conference on Research and Developments in Aeronautical Engineering”.
  7. Create a new submissionClick “Create New Submission”.
  8. Submit your paperEnter the required details, upload your full paper and complete the submission.
