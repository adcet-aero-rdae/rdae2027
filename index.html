ABOUT RDAE 2027

Research. Innovation. Collaboration.

The International Conference on Research and Developments in Aeronautical Engineering (RDAE 2027) aims to bring together researchers, academicians, industry professionals and students to share knowledge, present innovations and discuss emerging trends and challenges in aeronautical engineering.

RDAE 2027 provides a platform for collaboration and exchange of ideas to accelerate research, foster innovation and contribute to a sustainable aerospace future.

PAPER SUBMISSION (via Microsoft CMT)

Submit your full paper through Microsoft CMT.

Submit Your Full Paper

Authors are invited to submit their full papers through the Microsoft Conference Management Toolkit (CMT). Please complete your author details carefully and upload the required paper file through the conference submission portal.

Go to CMT Submission Portal →

The conference will use Microsoft CMT for managing the paper submission and peer-review process.

How to Submit Your Paper

  1. Create a CMT accountVisit cmt3.research.microsoft.com and create your account.
  2. Verify your accountComplete the email verification process.
  3. Log in to CMTSign in using your verified account.
  4. Find the conferenceClick “All Conferences” and search/filter for RDAE2027.
  5. Select the conferenceOpen “International Conference on Research and Developments in Aeronautical Engineering”.
  6. Create a new submissionClick “Create New Submission”.
  7. Submit your paperEnter the required details, upload your full paper and complete the submission.
